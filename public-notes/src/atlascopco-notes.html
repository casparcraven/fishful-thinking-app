Transformation one · The BT Global Challenge

What am I not seeing?

Leading the fleet, then crashing. We thought the answer to uncertainty was to push harder. We became burnt out, and people felt unseen, unheard, unappreciated. The crash forced a fundamental rethink: a fast boat is a happy boat became a happy boat is a fast boat.

The old chart: knowing
Certainty.
Follow me. Do this.
The new chart: learning
Curiosity.
What am I not seeing?
Put people first and the results will follow.

When Satya Nadella took over, Microsoft went from a company of know-it-alls to a company of learn-it-alls. And in your own words: the one closest to the problem is the one closest to the solution.

Transformation two · The business

How aligned is our team?

Ten years later I did exactly the same thing in my own company: worked harder, went faster, alone. Until a friend said: carry on like this and you'll lose all of us. The values conversation I rolled my eyes at changed everything. The team wrote the values, not me.

The old chart: silos
Go fast. Go alone.
The new chart: aligned
Go far. Go together.
If you want to go fast, go alone. If you want to go far, go together.
African proverb

Google studied 180 teams. The best weren't the ones that avoided mistakes. They were the ones where people felt safe enough to admit them early. Aligned doesn't mean everyone agrees. It means everyone feels safe enough to say what they see. Values shape behaviours, behaviours become actions, actions create outcomes. Yours: collaborate across boundaries, innovate to find better solutions, commit to delivering what you promise.

Your leadership model · Enterprise Leadership

Across boundaries. Growth mindset. Developing people.

Transformation three · Home

Impossible, or just hard?

No money, no boat, a wife seasick both times she'd sailed, three crew who hadn't chosen it. Nine reasons it couldn't be done. Every one of them was an opinion until we tested it.

The old chart: avoiding
That's impossible.
The new chart: experimenting
That's just hard.
Test it. Build the capability.
Impossible is usually an opinion, not a fact.

Two tests. Does it defy physics? Has someone, somewhere, done something similar? If no and yes, it's not impossible. It's just hard. Every "we can't" in a company is a capability nobody has been asked to build yet.

Your leadership model

Results and people. Complexity and change. The long term.
