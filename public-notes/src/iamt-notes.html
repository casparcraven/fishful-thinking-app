Transformation one · The BT Global Challenge

What am I not seeing?

Leading the fleet, then crashing. We had it the wrong way round: numbers over people, win at all costs. People felt unseen, unheard, unappreciated.

The old chart
Results first.
A fast boat is a happy boat.
The new chart
People first.
A happy boat is a fast boat.
Put people first and the results will follow.

Google studied 180 teams. The best weren't the ones that avoided mistakes. They were the ones where people felt safe enough to admit them early. Accountability starts with people telling you the truth.

How aligned is our team?

Transformation three · Home

Impossible, or just hard?

No money, no boat, a wife seasick both times she'd sailed, three crew who hadn't chosen it. Nine reasons it couldn't be done. Every one of them was an opinion until we tested it.

The old chart
That's impossible.
The new chart
That's just hard.
Build the capability.
Impossible is usually an opinion, not a fact.

Two tests. Does it defy physics? Has someone, somewhere, done something similar? If no and yes, it's not impossible. It's just hard. Every "we can't" in a company is a capability nobody has been asked to build yet. And when the pressure is on, culture reveals itself: it's not the resources we have, it's how resourceful we are.
